 A.Learning::
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
 1)Online Classes
      Use: Students attend classes online.
      Advantage: Students can learn from home.
      Disadvantage: Internet problems can disturb learning.
 2)Educational Videos
      Use: Students watch videos to understand lessons.
      Advantage: Makes learning easier.
      Disadvantage: Students may waste time watching unrelated videos.
B.Communication
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
     1)Email
    Use: Teachers send notes and assignments through email.
    Advantage: Fast communication.
    Disadvantage: Emails can contain spam.
    2)Video Conferencing
     Use: Teachers and students communicate online.
     Advantage: Saves travelling time.
     Disadvantage: Requires a good internet connection.
C.Managment 
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
     1)Student Records
     Use: Schools store student information digitally.
     Advantage: Easy to find records.
     Disadvantage: Data can be lost due to technical problems.
     2)Online Attendance
     Use: Teachers record students’ attendance digitally.
     Advantage: Saves time.
     Disadvantage: Technical errors may occur.
D. Entertainment
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
     1)Educational Games
     Use: Students play learning games.
     Advantage: Makes learning interesting.
     Disadvantage: Can waste time.
     2)Watching Movies
     Use: Students watch educational movies.
     Advantage: Provides knowledge in an interesting way.
Disadvantage: Too much screen time can affect productivity.
