                                                 <====Example of input devices====>
1)Key Board  (Both wire and wireless)            (used to type input and commands to pc )
2)Mouse      (Both wire and wireless)            (used to point,click and navigate on screen )
3)Scanner     (wired)                            (used to convert physical document into digital file )
4)Micro Phone (Both wire and wireless)           (used to capture audio)
5)Web Cam     ( wired)				 (used to capture photos and videos)
                    
                                                 <=====Example of output devices====>

1)Monitor      (wired)                           (used to display visual output like image and text)
2)Speaker      (Both wire and wireless)          (used to play audio)
3)Printer      (wired)                           (used to digital file into physical document )
4)Projector    (wired)                           (used to projects computer image on large screen)
5)Headphones   (Both wire and wireless)          (used to listen audio privately)
                        
                                                 <=====Example of storage devices====>

1)USB           (wireless)                       (used to transfer small files)
2)CD            (wireless)                       (used to store media and software)
3)Memorycard    (wireless)                       (used to store media)
4)SSD           (wired)                          (high speed portable storage for largebackup)
5)HDD           (wired                           (internal drive that store  the operating system)
